## Reading it

- **Goals in band.** `attackDamp` pulls the run average into 2.2–3.2 — out of the ~2× hot
  band the match-level report still shows. Early cups run higher (you stomp minnows), late
  cups lower (grinds), which is the realistic shape; the average is what the band governs.
- **Difficulty scales.** Cups 1→4 fall as a clean curve (opener winnable, final hard) as the
  opponent power curve overtakes the modelled deck growth — the Phase-4 "opponent scales" goal.

## Known balance concern (first pass — tuning ongoing)

- **The cup-5 boss is a wall for the sim AI** (~3% win, player barely scores). At the top of
  the power curve the opponent's per-sector DEFENCE matches the damped attack in every lane,
  so the threshold (pinned at 5) yields no chances — the same hot/wall tension the engine
  report documents, surfacing at the extreme. Two mitigations soften it (attack damp tuned to
  not over-cool; `powerKnee` compression on the boss finals), and the high draw rate (~31%)
  keeps most cup-5 TIES survivable (a draw continues the run). A real player can concentrate
  attack into one lane to break the wall — a lever the crude sub-only AI here never uses — so
  this understates human winnability. A deeper fix (opponent formation variety / a defence-side
  lever / a V6-native top-end power recalibration) is the next balance pass, not this one.
- First-pass instrument; the vitest gate locks only sane bands. Re-tune the `v6-bridge.ts` knobs
  here before touching engine constants — the handoff pins the threshold at 5.
