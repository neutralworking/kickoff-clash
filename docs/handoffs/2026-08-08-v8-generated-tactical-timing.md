# V8 generated Tactical timing pass — 2026-08-08

## Why this pass happened

The first 1,152-match calibration matrix exposed a rules-contract problem rather than a balance-number problem:

- player On Reveal effects resolve after the commitment window has closed;
- generated Tacticals therefore cannot actually be played in that same period;
- Park Ji-sung's THREE LUNGS generated a Trigger Press that was marked free only in the already-closed period;
- reveal/end-of-period generation in P4 left dead Tactical cards in hand after the final commitment window.

The goal of this pass was to define those semantics before changing Energy, Costs, printed stats or Action/Tactical values.

## Contract now used by the playable V8 calibration layer

V8 keeps **one commitment window per period**. No post-reveal action phase was added.

1. A Tactical generated by a player **On Reveal** is banked for the **next period's commitment window**.
2. A Tactical generated by an **end-of-period** effect is available in the next period's commitment window.
3. A Tactical generated by **movement during commitment** remains immediately playable that period. PENDOLINO is the key example.
4. Reveal/end-of-period Tactical generation in **P4 fizzles at FT** because no later commitment window exists. The runtime emits an explicit event instead of silently leaving an unusable card in hand.
5. Banked cards carry `availableFromPeriod`; the canonical V8 play/spend wrappers reject attempts to use them early.
6. THREE LUNGS now reads in the V8 calibration surface: `On Reveal: Add a Trigger Press to your hand for next period. It costs 0.` Its free period travels with the card to the first window where it can actually be played.

This is a timing fix, not a balance-number change.

## Tests updated

The integration suite now proves:

- BEND IT + Wambach banks the Cross in P1 and resolves the expected +8 ATT in P2;
- Wambach + Hegerberg still produces the +12 uncancellable Cross when the banked card is played;
- PAUSE AND SLIP + Shevchenko still produces the expected +8 Through Ball in its legal next window;
- Neymar/Panenka Penalty payoff is banked and playable next period;
- Baresi banks Offside Trap and can cancel the next-period Through Ball;
- THREE LUNGS is unavailable in the reveal period, then costs 0 in the next commitment window;
- PENDOLINO-generated Cross is immediately playable because the movement happened during commitment;
- THUNDERBALL / WHIPPED DELIVERY generation respects the next-window rule;
- P4 reveal generation and P4 KILLER PASS generation fizzle instead of leaving dead cards.

The focused V8 gate passes with these semantics.

## What this tells us

The timing bug was materially affecting balance observations. Through Ball roughly doubled its win rate and deployed more of its XI once Park's generated Trigger Press became free in the window where it can actually be used. Control's dominance also reduced because several opposing squads contain Park while Control / Defence does not.

This reinforces the calibration sequence: do not buff Through Ball or nerf Control yet.

The remaining structural problems are still clear:

- Control / Defence remains strongest at roughly 74% wins.
- Balanced / Midrange is also strong at roughly 64%.
- Through Ball remains weakest at roughly 18%, with 3.87 unused Energy and only 6.66 players deployed.
- Dribbling / Penalty still plays **zero Penalty Tacticals** in the non-self squad sample, so its result is not yet a Penalty-mechanic test.
- Cross remains the most consistently exercised specialist package.
- Long Shot / Set Piece remains late-game DEF-heavy and low-scoring.
