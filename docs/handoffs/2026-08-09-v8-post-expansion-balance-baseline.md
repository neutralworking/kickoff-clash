## Important: this baseline was taken twice

The first pass of this measurement was run at `336ab3126095d8fa80effd957bf68d0817f3368b` (the
Batch 07 documentation commit) and found the six-reference-XI matrix an exact match to previously
accepted evidence — see git history for that draft if needed. Before it could be pushed, two
commits landed upstream on this same branch:

1. **`707b43a` — V8 mobile match UI and game-feel pass.** UI-only; no engine/balance surface.
2. **`5262c66` — Remove the same-period Generated-Tactical Window from live play.** Owner-directed:
   a Tactical generated by playing a player is now usable on a subsequent turn, never the turn it
   was generated, in any period including P4. Full detail in
   `docs/handoffs/2026-08-10-v8-generated-tactical-window-removed.md`.

The window removal is a live-play rules change, not a card-content or Cost change, and it measurably
moves the six-reference-XI matrix (see below). Publishing the first-pass numbers as "the baseline"
would have been actively wrong the moment they landed, since they described a mechanic that no
longer exists in live play on this branch. This document was re-measured in full against the new
head rather than pushed as originally drafted.

**No changes were made by this pass to any card, Cost, scoring rule, Energy curve, or sequencing.**
It is a measurement pass only, run twice, over a branch that moved once underneath it.

## Six-reference-XI matrix: before vs. after the window removal

This is the controlled reference cohort (the original 30-card calibration pool, unchanged by
Batches 01–07). Nothing about the cards moved between these two columns — only the live-play rule
of when a generated Tactical becomes playable did.

| Squad | W (window live, pre-removal) | W (no window, current) | Δ | GD (pre) | GD (current) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cross | 48% | 49% | +1 | +0.172 | +0.322 |
| Through Ball | 42% | **31%** | **-11** | +0.009 | **-0.641** |
| Dribbling / Penalty | 48% | **54%** | +6 | +0.419 | +0.825 |
| Control / Defence | 35% | 43% | +8 | -0.325 | +0.041 |
| Long Shot / Set Piece | 33% | **25%** | **-8** | -0.581 | **-0.972** |
| Balanced / Midrange | 49% | 50% | +1 | +0.306 | +0.425 |

The current-column numbers match exactly what `5262c66` itself committed
(`test-results/v8-calibration-matrix.txt` — byte-identical on re-run, confirming the simulator is
deterministic and this branch's tracked evidence is accurate and current).

**One thing worth the owner's attention that the removal commit's own message didn't call out:**
it names Long Shot/Set Piece, Cross and Dribbling/Penalty as the families that moved, but
**Through Ball dropped from 42% to 31%** (GD from +0.009 to -0.641) — a bigger swing in win-rate
terms than Long Shot/Set Piece's drop, and Through Ball's GD went from roughly break-even to
clearly negative. This is a mechanical consequence of the same fix (Through Ball's compact-core
package also leaned on same-period window plays, per the old evidence: `window 1.122 plays / 0.122
E / 1.541 ATT` before removal, now `0 plays / 0 E / 0 ATT`), not a new bug, but it's a second
family now sitting below the ~40% competitive line the earlier broad-validation work used, not
just one.

## A gap this surfaced: broad-panel diagnostics no longer describe live play

The broad deck-validation panel (54 decks / 5,184 matches), compact-core sensitivity, and
minimal-Cross sensitivity harnesses were re-run at the new head and are **byte-identical** to the
first pass — completely unaffected by the window removal. This is expected, not a bug: per
`5262c66`'s own handoff, these harnesses (`calibration-deck-validation.ts`,
`calibration-compact-core.ts`, `calibration-cross-minimal.ts`) call the window-resolution API
directly in their own hand-rolled match sequences, separate from the live matchup-matrix simulator
that the two removed call sites lived in. They still simulate same-period window plays that live
gameplay can no longer produce.

Practically: the broad panel currently shows Long Shot/Set Piece at a healthy 44% median (9–64%
range, 6/9 competitive) and Through Ball at 42% median (6–68% range, 5/9 competitive) — both of
which look fine. But the controlled six-XI matrix, which does reflect live play, now shows those
same two families at 25% and 31%. Reading the broad panel alone would miss that these two families
are meaningfully weaker in the game a player actually plays post-removal. This isn't something to
fix by editing the harnesses right now — it's a fact worth recording so a future balance pass
doesn't get misled by the broad panel's now-stale rule assumptions.

## Batch 01–07 expansion content

Unaffected. None of the 53 runtime-ready expansion cards or the 6 mixed integration XIs are part

simulator and the interactive Lab — not card definitions, Actions, or generation effects (the

## What this pass is and isn't recommending

**Not recommending any immediate stat/Cost change.** This pass's job was to measure and report,
not to react to a same-day rules change with reactive tuning. The window removal was explicit
owner direction, already verified and merged with its own full test suite; second-guessing it is
out of scope here.

**Flagging for the next balance-focused pass:** Long Shot/Set Piece (25%, GD -0.972) and Through
Ball (31%, GD -0.641) are now the two clearly sub-competitive families in the controlled matrix,
where before the removal only Long Shot/Set Piece sat meaningfully below the pack. Both families'
old evidence shows they leaned on same-period window plays for a meaningful share of their ATT
(Long Shot most heavily: `1.328 plays / 0.297 E / 8.044 ATT` pre-removal). Per the standing
structural-fix-before-stat-tuning hierarchy, the first question for that pass should be whether
either package has a non-window structural lever (dependency density, trigger frequency, timing)
before touching raw ATT/DEF/Cost — not an immediate buff.

PR #106 remains open, draft, unmerged. No cards, Cost values, scoring rules, Energy curve, or
sequencing were changed by this pass.
