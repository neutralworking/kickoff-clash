# Decision: expanded player view format

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Status:** provisional / accepted for now  
**Parent spec:** `docs/player-card-system-spec.md`

The expanded player view will use a **full-screen mobile dossier** rather than a small modal.

## Structure

- premium 2:3 player card as the opening hero object
- pinned close/back control above the content
- scroll or swipe into the supporting player information
- full action name, trigger, effect and target
- primary and secondary eligible positions
- role/archetype as descriptive identity only
- career record
- optional future condition module, without reserving permanent space now

## Principles

- the expanded view must feel like the same card elevated, not a disconnected database inspector
- the portrait, KC monogram, nameplate, rarity frame and ATT/DEF treatment must remain visually continuous with smaller card variants
- gameplay-critical information appears first; flavour and record follow
- no fitness or durability sections
- exact layout and interaction remain open to revision during visual prototyping
